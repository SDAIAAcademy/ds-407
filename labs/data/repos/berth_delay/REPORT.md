# Slot booking at T2: effect on berth waiting

Prepared by the analytics unit for the director of marine operations. Draft for review.

## Finding

Slot booking has **halved** waiting at T2. In months 13 to 24, vessels at T2 waited 7.5 hours on average against
12.9 hours at the other terminals. Waiting at T2 also fell from 10.7 to 7.5 hours after slot booking started.

## Recommendation

Extend slot booking to all four terminals from next quarter.

## Method

Berth calls of months 1 to 24 (berth_calls.csv). Calls without a waiting time were removed as incomplete records.
