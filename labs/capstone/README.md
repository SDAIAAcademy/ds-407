# Capstone: a quality-assurance pack for the night pilot staffing analysis

Saleh Al-Ghamlas has analysed the night shift of the authority's marine pilots and recommends cutting it from six
pilots to four. Bader Al-Khayyal, director of marine operations, wants to take the recommendation to the board. You
are the head of the analytics unit. Before anything goes to the board, you assure the analysis.

The practice version is in `data/`: `staffing.py` (the analysis) and `pilot_jobs.csv` (a year of synthetic night
pilotage jobs). The trainer gives each participant an assessed variant of the same analysis; the variants are not
published.

## What you submit

A public GitHub repository holding four documents and the commit history that produced them:

1. **The applied standard** (`standard.md`): the assurance level of this analysis under the unit's standard, with the
   grades of effect and complexity, the escalators that apply, and the roles (analyst, assurer, approver).
2. **The review report** (`review.md`): the re-run log, the findings, each rated by its effect on the decision, and
   what must change before the board sees it.
3. **The analysis agreement** (`agreement.md`): the question, the options, the decision rule and an evidence
   threshold the data can detect, with roles and sign-off.
4. **The board quality statement** (`quality_statement.md`): what was checked, by whom, what remains uncertain with a
   number, any assisted part, the classification. At most 160 words.

Use an assistant if you wish (AI-full): disclose where, and keep the prompts in the repository. You defend every line
yourself in the meeting.

## The review meeting (15 minutes)

You chair a fifteen-minute review meeting with two role players: the analyst, who defends his recommendation, and the
sponsor, who wants it on the board's agenda. You open, take the meeting through your findings, agree what happens
next, and close with a decision and its record. During the meeting the sponsor presses once: a date, a finding he
wants softened, or a step he wants skipped. An observer scores five criteria from 0 to 2: the opening (purpose,
level, the decision to reach), the findings (decision-changing ones first, with evidence), the analyst (hears the
defence, accepts what is right, holds what is not), the sponsor's pressure (answered with options, finding and level
kept), and the close (a decision, actions with owners, the record).

## Rubric

| Part | Weight |
|---|---|
| Standard | 20 |
| Review findings | 30 |
| Analysis agreement | 20 |
| Quality statement | 20 |
| Clarity | 10 |

The pack is half of the course assessment and the chaired meeting is 30%; the supervised review without AI
assistance is the other 20%. Passing is 70% overall and at least 60% on each component.
