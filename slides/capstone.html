The supervised check: one analysis, 40 minutes, no AI

  • A short analysis you have not seen, with seeded flaws
  • Re-run it, list the findings and rate each by its effect on the decision
  • Marked against the trainer's key: the findings that change the decision count most

Laptops offline apart from the analysis and a text editor; no assistant, no tutor. The analysis and its key are in the trainer's private exam folder, a new one per cohort. Mark with the severity scale of the unit's standard (S-5): a privacy finding rated minor loses its marks, as in Lab 2.1.

Four documents and their commit history, in a public repository

The level of this analysis: effect, complexity, escalators, and the roles

The re-run log and the findings, rated by effect on the decision

agreement.md

Question, options, decision rule, a threshold the data can detect, sign-off

For the board, at most 160 words, with uncertainty as a number

AI-full is allowed in the pack: the README says where an assistant was used and the prompts are kept in the repository, the rules of Lab 1.4 applied to themselves. The commit history is inspected for authenticity: a pack that arrives in one commit is a question for the meeting.

Variants and keys are prepared by the trainer outside the repository; the practice version's flaws are in the answer key and must not be used for assessment. Hand the variants out as files, one per participant, and record who had which.

Full marks, one example each: the safety escalator applied, so level 4 with a second reviewer; the finding that flips the decision listed first (the mean in place of the busy nights); a rule with a threshold the variant's data can detect, agreed before the result; a statement with a range and the disclosure line; documents a board secretary could read without the analyst present.

The result

Three parts make the result, each with its own floor

PartWeight
Supervised check, without AI20%
The pack, on the rubric50%
The chaired review meeting, observed30%
To pass: 70% overall, and at least 60% on each part.

The floors stop a strong pack from carrying a weak meeting, or the reverse. The meeting is where the decisions in the pack are shown to be the participant's.

Fifteen minutes

You chair; the analyst defends; the sponsor wants it on the agenda

Open · 2 min

Purpose, the level, what the meeting must decide

Findings · 8 min

The findings that change the decision first; hear the analyst on each

Close · 5 min

The decision, what changes before the board, who does what, the record

During the meeting the sponsor presses: a date, a finding he wants softened, or a reason to skip a step. Chair through it.

The role players have briefs from the trainer's folder. The sponsor's pressure comes at about minute six, one sentence in Bader's voice; vary it between participants. The analyst defends the mean as "what we always use".

Practise it now

A practice push from the sponsor: what do you say?

Bader Al-KhayyalThe board meets this month. Can we take the recommendation as it is and fix the details afterwards?
  • Which finding makes "as it is" impossible, and why?
  • What can go to this month's board, and at which level?
  • What goes in the record of the meeting?

The meetings use other pushes. Give pairs five minutes: one plays Bader, one answers in two minutes. The weak answer refuses; the strong one offers what can go this month (the busy-night analysis, the tanker season) and keeps the recommendation off until it is reviewed at level 4.

Before the first meeting

Fifteen minutes: open your repository as a stranger would

Open the public link in a private window. Are the four documents there, and could the board read your statement alone?

A private window catches a private repository and a missing push, the two most common reasons a pack cannot be marked.

Now chairing

Review meeting

Start the timer when the participant opens. Signal the sponsor's push at about minute six. Questions to keep ready if the meeting stalls: what would the board need to see to approve four pilots? Who assures this at level 4? What goes in the record?

After the course

The follow-up task, and what comes next

  • Follow-up task. Within six weeks, apply your checklist to one real analysis in your unit with a second reviewer, and bring the agreement table and the sentence you changed; keep personal and restricted data out of anything you share.
  • Keep the repository. It is evidence for the DS Expert badge.
  • Back to the start. Reviewing one analysis in depth is DS-310; send your new analysts there.

The catalog names a tracked follow-up task for this format but not its wording; confirm the task and its deadline with the programme office before the cohort, and replace the first bullet if it differs.
